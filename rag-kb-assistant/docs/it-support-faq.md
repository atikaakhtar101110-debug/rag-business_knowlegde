# IT Support FAQ

## How do I reset my password?

Open the self-service portal and choose "Forgot password". You will receive a one-time code on your registered phone. If you no longer have access to that phone, contact the IT service desk with your employee ID.

## How do I connect to the VPN?

Install the company VPN client from the software portal, sign in with your work email, and approve the multi-factor prompt. The VPN is required to reach internal tools when working outside the office.

## What should I do if my laptop is lost or stolen?

Report it to the IT service desk within one hour. They will lock the device remotely and issue a replacement. Also file a report with building security if it was lost on company premises.

## How do I request new software?

Submit a request in the software portal. Requests under 500 USD per year are approved by your department head; larger requests also need approval from the IT security team.
