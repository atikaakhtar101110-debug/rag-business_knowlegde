# Company Handbook

## Working hours

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees agree their remaining hours with their manager. Remote work is allowed up to three days per week without special approval.

## Expense policy

### Travel

Book flights at least 14 days in advance when possible. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 150 USD per night in standard cities and 220 USD per night in high-cost cities.

### Meals and small purchases

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Receipts are required for any purchase above 25 USD. Submit all expense reports within 30 days of the purchase date.

| Expense type | Limit | Approver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hotel (standard city) | 150 USD per night | Line manager |
| Hotel (high-cost city) | 220 USD per night | Line manager |
| Meals | 60 USD per day | Line manager |
| Software subscriptions | 500 USD per year | Department head |

## Security basics

Use the company password manager for every work account. Report a lost laptop or phone to the IT service desk within one hour. Never share customer data over personal messaging apps.
